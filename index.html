Edwina Sullivan has spent nearly two decades leading business development and marketing at some of the world's most respected professional services firms across Australia and Asia Pacific.

She has led BD teams in Melbourne and Singapore and supported practices across the region — working directly alongside partners, practice group leaders, and management committees to develop and execute growth strategies that actually get implemented.
