About Edwina

Senior expertise,
personally delivered

Edwina is a senior business development and marketing leader with nearly two decades of experience at some of the world's leading law firms, including Herbert Smith Freehills, Freshfields, Baker McKenzie, and Kennedys.

She has worked across every aspect of BD and marketing — from strategy and pursuits to client programs, communications, and team leadership — and has spent the majority of her career operating at a regional level across Asia Pacific, including almost a decade based in Singapore.

At Sullivan Jade Advisory, you work with Edwina directly. Her advice is grounded in real experience, from inside the firms and markets she now helps others navigate.
