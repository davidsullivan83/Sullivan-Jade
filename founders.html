For law firm founders

Practical business development and marketing support for sole practitioners and small to medium-sized partnerships.

Starting your own firm means building more than a legal practice. You are also building a brand, a reputation and a way for clients to find you.

Whether you are planning your launch, have recently opened, or want more structure in place, Sullivan Jade Advisory helps you put the foundations in place.

Who this is for

  • Lawyers planning on establishing a sole practice or a small partnership
  • Founders who have recently launched and want a clearer approach to winning work and building profile
  • Existing small firms that have grown by word of mouth and now want a more deliberate approach to their business development and marketing

How we can help

Fixed scope

Launch Kit

A focused, fixed-scope program to take you to market with clarity. We work through your positioning, your messaging and your launch plan, and prepare the materials to support the launch of your business.

90 days

Pipeline Foundations

A 90-day program to build the habits and systems that support a sustainable approach to winning work and retaining clients.

Ongoing

Your Business Development Partner

Flexible support that grows and changes with your firm.
